FIELD AGENT

Fifteen destinations. Ten missions each. Question witnesses, sort truth from red herrings, then head to the scene to find your target — before your time runs out.

Difficulty

Move with WASD / Arrow Keys (or the on-screen pad on mobile).

How to Play

1. Briefing. Agent Vale tells you who or what is missing — or, in a murder case, who's suspected.

2. Question witnesses. Four questions, five answers each. Only one answer per question is true — the rest are red herrings. The true answer also gives a small deduction hint.

3. Suspect lineup (murder cases only). Pick the right suspect from a lineup before heading out. Pick wrong and the case is blown.

4. Head to the scene. Good answers highlight a zone on the map and clear out false leads. Bad answers mean a crowded scene full of decoys — touch the wrong glowing lead, or get hit by traffic, and the mission fails instantly.

5. Shop. Spend coins on permanent speed/time/radius upgrades, a cosmetic look in the Wardrobe, or a Distress Flare — a one-time-use item you trigger mid-mission to reveal the real lead.
